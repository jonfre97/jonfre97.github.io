Ciao, I'm Jonas!

Welcome to my personal website (which is clearly under construction). I am an astroparticle physicist currently based at the Physics Department of Sapienza University of Rome. My research focuses on the overlap between particle physics, cosmology and astrophysics. Currently, I am mostly working on light and ultralight (WISPy) physics beyond the Standard Model, in particular as part of Andrea Caputo's ERC StG grant AstroDarkLS. Furthermore, I have a strong interest in Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, in particular when considered as a tool to constrain BSM physics.

I have spent the time of my PhD in Hamburg working at DESY under supervision of Kai Schmidt-Hoberg. In 2024, I have obtained my doctorate from the University of Hamburg. The thesis focused on constraints on dark photons arising from solar production and new ways to detect B-L dark photon dark matter.

For my Bachelor and Master degree, I studied in Aachen at the RWTH. Both theses were supervised by Felix Kahlhoefer who introduced me to both the world of academia and the fascination of hunting dark matter.
